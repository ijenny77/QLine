1. PRODUCT PURPOSE
   ==================================================

QLine is a digital queue management system for hospitals, banks and government/public-service offices.

Core idea:

"Your place in line, without standing in line."

A citizen should be able to:

1. Choose a service.
2. Join a queue using a phone.
3. Receive a ticket number and estimated waiting time.
4. Leave the waiting area.
5. Receive a notification when their turn is approaching.
6. Return and be served.
7. Track their position.

The institution should be able to:

1. See the live queue.
2. Register walk-ins.
3. Call the next person.
4. Mark people as served.
5. Handle late arrivals.
6. See basic queue statistics.
7. Continue operating during a simulated connectivity outage.

This is a PRODUCT DEMO, not a marketing website.
